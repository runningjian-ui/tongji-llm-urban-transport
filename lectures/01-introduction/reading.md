# 第 1 讲 阅读材料

## 📚 必读

### 1. Nie, T., Sun, J., & Ma, W. (2025)
**Exploring the roles of large language models in reshaping transportation systems: A survey, framework, and roadmap**

*Artificial Intelligence for Transportation*, 1, 100003

- **推荐章节**：前 3 章 + 第 5 章（LLM 角色分类）
- **为什么读**：本课程最对口的综述，描述了 LLM 在交通的 4 类角色
- **链接**：[ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/artificial-intelligence-for-transportation)

### 2. Choi, S., Jin, Z., Ham, S. W., Kim, J., & Sun, L. (2025)
**A gentle introduction and tutorial on deep generative models in transportation research**

*Transportation Research Part C*, 176, 105145

- **推荐章节**：第 1-2 章（导论）+ 教程部分
- **为什么读**：UMN 课程的配套论文，含可运行代码
- **链接**：[GitHub](https://github.com/UMN-Choi-Lab/DGMinTransportation)

### 3. Vaswani et al. (2017)
**Attention Is All You Need**

*NeurIPS 2017*

- **推荐章节**：全文
- **为什么读**：理解 LLM 的基础架构
- **链接**：[arXiv:1706.03762](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)

## 📖 选读

### 4. Park, J. et al. (2023)
**Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior**

*UIST 2023 (Best Paper)*

- **为什么读**：第 7 讲"多智能体"将深入讨论
- **链接**：[arXiv:2304.03442](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03442)

### 5. OpenAI (2022)
**Introducing ChatGPT**

- **链接**：[博客](https://openai.com/blog/chatgpt)
- **为什么读**：理解 Chat 模型的能力边界

### 6. OpenAI (2024)
**Learning to Reason with LLMs (o1)**

- **链接**：[博客](https://openai.com/index/learning-to-reason-with-llms/)
- **为什么读**：第 3 讲"推理模型"将深入讨论
